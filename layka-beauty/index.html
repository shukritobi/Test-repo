BUILD NOTES

What is real vs placeholder?

Did research verify a cosmetics company owned by Layka?

No. Research confirmed the creator, audience and beauty activity, but not a registered Layka-owned cosmetics company. This prototype avoids inventing a legal entity.

Is “Lipbalm Gentil” confirmed as her own product?

No. It appears in the supplied Instagram screenshot, but indexed Instagram results also show Layka promoting Glad2Glow lip balm. That is not enough to establish ownership.

Can this become a real store?

Yes. The site can stay lightweight on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages, then connect to verified catalogue data, a payment gateway, marketplace links, WhatsApp orders or a Cloudflare backend.
