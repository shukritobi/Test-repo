Frequently asked

Everything before
the first spray.

How do customers order?

Customers add items to the bag, enter their details, then proceed to a payment gateway or WhatsApp-assisted checkout. The final production flow can follow ASFT's preferred operation.

Can this connect to Meta ads?

Yes. Meta Pixel, ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout and Purchase events can be implemented so campaigns can optimize against actual customer behavior.

Can there be different landing pages?

Yes. We can create a focused page for each scent, bundle, creator campaign or promotion while keeping all sales and analytics under one domain.

Can products and prices be changed later?

Yes. The catalogue is structured so final names, notes, bottle sizes, images, pricing and stock can be replaced once ASFT shares the confirmed product list.
